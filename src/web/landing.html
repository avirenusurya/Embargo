DECLASSIFYING FILE 0006 · CONSENT
06CONSENTPER SPEAKER · PER LINENO ACCOUNT NEEDED

Their words.Their call.

Guests, customers and partners say things in your meetings. Before any AI tool reuses a sensitive line, the person who said it decides.

  1. 01HeldLines that mention prices, deals, personal details or outsiders are held. Everything else is allowed automatically.
  2. 02LinkEach speaker with held lines gets a single-use review link on Slack, WhatsApp or email.
  3. 03DecideThey see only their own lines: Allow, Team only, Block from AI, or embargo a line until a date.
  4. 04RecordEach decision gets a signed record of what was decided and when. The record never holds the words.

Lines nobody reviews by the deadline fall back to the workspace default, which is blocked unless you change it.

  3. Purge embeddings, facts and cached compiles

  5. Write revoke and key_destroyed records
